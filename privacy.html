响指计划隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

响指计划（Planck）由个人开发者沈世博（Shibo Shen）开发和提供。我们尽可能少地接触你的信息。

1. 我们不收集你的数据

你在响指计划里创建的行动、标签、昵称、完成记录等内容只保存在你的设备上。本 App 没有自己的服务器，不接入统计、广告或第三方追踪服务，不使用广告标识符。

2. iCloud 备份

你可以在「设置」里打开 iCloud 备份。备份写入你自己 Apple 账号下的 iCloud 私有数据库，只有你能访问，开发者无法查看。你可以随时关闭备份，或在 iCloud 设置中删除。

3. 小组件

App 通过设备上的 App 群组与自己的小组件共享一份摘要（如行动数量、要务标题），这份数据不会离开你的设备。

4. 购买

终身版通过 App Store 购买，付款由 Apple 处理，开发者拿不到你的支付信息，只能得知购买是否生效。

5. 我们会收到的信息

只有你主动发给我们的邮件和反馈。它们只用于回复你和改进响指计划。根据你在设备上的设置，Apple 可能向开发者提供匿名的崩溃报告；参与 TestFlight 测试时，Apple 也会按其 TestFlight 隐私说明向开发者提供测试信息。

6. 删除你的数据

删除 App 即删除设备上的全部内容；iCloud 备份可在系统设置中删除。如需删除你发给我们的邮件或反馈，请来信告知。

7. 儿童

响指计划不专门面向儿童，也不收集儿童信息。

8. 政策变更

如果我们开始收集任何数据，会先更新这份政策和 App Store 隐私标签。

Planck Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Planck is developed and provided by Shibo Shen, an independent developer. We keep as little of your information as we can.

1. We don't collect your data

Actions, tags, your nickname and completion history stay on your device. The app has no server of its own, no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking, and does not use the advertising identifier.

2. iCloud Backup

You can turn on iCloud Backup in Settings. It writes to the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account, which only you can access; the developer cannot see it. You can turn it off or delete it in iCloud settings at any time.

3. Widgets

The app shares a summary (such as counts and priority titles) with its own widget through an on-device App Group. It does not leave your device.

4. Purchases

The Lifetime tier is bought through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; the developer receives no payment details, only whether the purchase is active.

5. What we receive

Only email and feedback you choose to send, used to reply to you and improve Planck. Depending on your device settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer; TestFlight testing information is shared as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice.

6. Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything on the device; iCloud Backup can be deleted in system settings. To have email or feedback you sent deleted, write to us.

7. Children

Planck is not directed at children and collects no information about them.

8. Changes

If we ever begin collecting data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label first.
